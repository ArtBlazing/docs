## How Cove Buy Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add to Group">
    Add [@cove_buy_bot](https://t.me/cove_buy_bot) to your group/channel and give admin rights.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Settings">
    Configure your Group Settings according to your community's needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Earning">
    Members see Quick Buy buttons under scanned tokens → they tap → trade executes through Cove. All referral fees go directly to your Cove account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Cove Buy is a user-facing tool. Your members don’t need to understand setup — they just tap Buy.
</Note>

## How to Set Up Cove Buy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the Bot">
    Go to [https://t.me/cove_buy_bot](https://t.me/cove_buy_bot) to open the bot in Telegram. Once you press start, it will load the default menu of Cove Bot. Open Cove Buy.
    <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/cove%20start%20menu.png" alt="Start Bot" /></Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add to your group/channel">
    <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/select%20group%20to%20where%20to%20add%20cove.png" alt="Select Group" /></Frame>
    <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/add%20cove%20to%20community_ok.png" alt="Add to Group OK" /></Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Give it an Admin role">
    Right click on the bot's profile and Promote to admin. Configure the rules and click **Save**. You can give it a Custom title if you want.
    <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/promote%20cove%20to%20admin.png" alt="Promote to Admin" /></Frame>
    <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/save%20admin%20rights.png" alt="Save Admin Rights" /></Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure your Group">
    Click **Configure Group**. It will redirect you to the Bot and Group Settings message will pop up.
    <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/cove%20successfully%20added.png" alt="Cove Successfully Added" /></Frame>
    <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/come%20main%20menu.png" alt="Main Menu" /></Frame>
    
    <Check>
      Cove Buy is now added to your community. To increase your user experience even more, you can configure your group in Bot Settings.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Custom Buy Amounts

You can set custom Buy Amounts for your group that better suit your members. Everyone in this group will see the same.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Group Settings">
    Use `/group_settings` command from Bot menu to launch your Group Settings. Here, click **Custom Buy Amounts**.
    <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/custom%20buy%20amounts%20menu.png" alt="Open Group Settings" /></Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select amount to change">
    Each chain has its own custom Buy Amounts. Make sure to select a proper chain before editing.
    <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/enter%20custom%20buy%20amount%20to%20change.png" alt="Select Amount" /></Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply the new amount">
    <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/enter%20amount%205.png" alt="Apply Amount" /></Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Buy amount is changed">
    Your group Buy Amounts have been changed successfully. Now your members will see the updated amount once you send a new contract address in your group.
    <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/changed%20buy%20amounts.png" alt="Amount Changed" /></Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Custom Links Setup

We understand that people prefer different trading bots. With Cove Buy, you decide which bots you want to keep as quick buy links, and all referral fees from these links will go straight to your account.

Cove Buy lets you set your own referral links for each scan in your community.

<Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/custom%20links%20setup%20menu.png" alt="Custom Links Setup" /></Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a chain to find a trading bot">
    <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/custom%20links%20menu_select%20other%20chain.png" alt="Select Chain" /></Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select trading bot">
    Tap on the trading bot name to launch a custom ref link setup menu.
    <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/custom%20links%20menu_sol%20chain%20selected.png" alt="Select Bot" /></Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Referral & Get your Referral code">
    Get your Referral code (referral username) from another trading bot.
    <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/set%20custom%20link%20beign.png" alt="Set Custom Link Begin" /></Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter this code to Cove Buy menu">
    Done. You've set a custom referral link. Although we set a custom link for the SOL chain, it will apply to all chains supported by the trading bot from your custom ref link.
    <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/custom%20links%20menu_referral%20set%20successfully.png" alt="Referral Set Successfully" /></Frame>
    <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/custom%20ref%20link%20in%20in%20scans.png" alt="Ref Link in Scans" /></Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
