Cove Buy turns every token scan in your group/channel into a 1-click buy experience (no copy-paste, no switching apps). Cove Buy is a Telegram tool that adds Quick Buy buttons under scanned tokens so your members can enter faster — and you can earn from the activity in your community.

## 🛠️ How to Set Up Cove Buy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the Bot">
    Go to [https://t.me/cove_buy_bot](https://t.me/cove_buy_bot) to open the bot in Telegram. Once you press start, it will load the default menu of Cove Bot. Open Cove Buy.
    <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/cove%20start%20menu.png" alt="Start Bot" /></Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add to your group/channel">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Select Group">
        <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/select%20group%20to%20where%20to%20add%20cove.png" alt="Select Group" /></Frame>
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Confirm">
        <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/add%20cove%20to%20community_ok.png" alt="Add to Group OK" /></Frame>
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Give it an Admin role">
    Right click on the bot's profile and Promote to admin. Configure the rules and click **Save**. You can give it a Custom title if you want.
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Promote to Admin">
        <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/promote%20cove%20to%20admin.png" alt="Promote to Admin" /></Frame>
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Save Rights">
        <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/save%20admin%20rights.png" alt="Save Admin Rights" /></Frame>
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure your Group">
    Use `/group_settings` to open the settings interface.
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Settings">
        <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/group%20settings.png" alt="Group Settings" /></Frame>
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Select Community">
        <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/group%20settings_community%20selected.png" alt="Community Selected" /></Frame>
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
    
    <Check>
      Cove Buy is now added to your community. To increase your user experience even more, you can configure your group in Bot Settings.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 💰 Custom Buy Amounts

You can set custom Buy Amounts for your group that better suit your members. Everyone in this group will see the same.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Group Settings">
    Use `/group_settings` command from Bot menu to launch your Group Settings. Here, click **Custom Buy Amounts**.
    <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/group%20settings_community%20selected_custom%20buy%20amount.png" alt="Open Group Settings" /></Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select amount to change">
    Each chain has its own custom Buy Amounts. Make sure to select a proper chain before editing.
    <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/group%20settings_community%20selected_custom%20buy%20amount_change.png" alt="Select Amount" /></Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply the new amount">
    Your group Buy Amounts have been changed successfully. Now your members will see the updated amount once you send a new contract address in your group.
    <Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/group%20settings_community%20selected_custom%20buy%20amount_change_updated.png" alt="Amount Changed" /></Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 🔗 Custom Links Setup

We understand that people prefer different trading bots. With Cove Buy, you decide which bots you want to keep as quick buy links, and all referral fees from these links will go straight to your account.

Cove Buy lets you set your own referral links for each scan in your community.

<Frame><img src="/assets/cove_buy/group%20settings_custom%20links.png" alt="Custom Links Setup" /></Frame>

<Steps>
