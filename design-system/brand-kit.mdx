## Logo

The Cove logo mark is a geometric shield derived from a singular block that has been softened and opened. The three distinct segments represent "coves" — protected inlets that offer shelter across different chains — while the rounded edges and deliberate negative space evoke a sense of accessibility and comfort.

### Logo Spacing

To ensure our logo always remains clear and legible, maintain a clear-space area around the logo equal to the full height/width of the Logo Mark. This is the **minimum** clear-space required — always use more when possible.

<Warning>
  Do **not** alter the logo — no stretching, recoloring, adding effects, or changing proportions.
</Warning>

## Brand Expression

### Cove-Flow

Cove-Flow is the engine driving the visual identity. It encompasses the liquid motion of assets moving across the bridgeless void — rendered in Azure and Resolution Blue to prove that complex trades can be smooth, direct, and effortless.

In motion, this energy becomes the visual heartbeat — shaping transitions and purposeful text appearances to maintain constant momentum. Closely imitating light caustics caused by flowing water, it instills video-based content with calm, seamless, yet progressive motion.

### Emboss Treatment

While the logo exists as a flat, precision-engineered SVG for core functionality, it should be **embossed in all brand communications.** Embossing the logo gives Cove identity a tactile, confident presence.

By moving beyond flat digital trends, this treatment creates a "refined and expensive" aesthetic that signals high-end engineering.

### Noise Texture

When using gradients or blurs, a subtle noise texture softens the image while adding grit.

**To recreate:**
1. Above your image, create a solid color layer — `#4D4D4D`
2. Apply noise filter at **400%** strength
3. Set the blending mode to **Soft Light**
4. Set the opacity to around **20%**

## Usage Rules

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do's" icon="circle-check">
    - ✅ Use logos with proper clear-space
    - ✅ Use on backgrounds that provide strong contrast
    - ✅ Use Plus Jakarta Sans for all headlines and body copy
    - ✅ Use Roboto Mono for code and numerical values
    - ✅ Apply gradients subtly — they should **enhance**, not overpower
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Don'ts" icon="circle-xmark">
    - ❌ Do not stretch, skew, or rotate the logo
    - ❌ Do not change the logo colors
    - ❌ Do not add effects (shadows, glows, outlines) to the logo
    - ❌ Do not place the logo on busy backgrounds without clear space
    - ❌ Do not use "meme-heavy" or overly casual visual styles
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
