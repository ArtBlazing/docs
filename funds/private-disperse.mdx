Private Disperse routes one payment through an exchange-routing provider, Husher, to fund your own wallets separately. Each destination can have its own amount, exchange, and delay.

<Note>This option appears in `/transfer` only where available. If it is absent, use standard [Disperse USDC](/features/transfers) or ask support about availability.</Note>

Choose `/transfer` → **Private Disperse** to open Cove Wallets, then follow its four steps.

## Follow progress

You can close the Mini App and follow Telegram notifications. Reopen the run for per-wallet **Queued**, **Scheduled**, **Sending**, **Funded**, **Failed**, or **Refunded** states.

**Send now** skips a destination's remaining delay. **Track on Husher** opens provider tracking. A partial completion means some destinations were funded while others still need attention.

For failed destinations, use **Get refund** to open support. Keep the run reference. Do not create a replacement run before reviewing completed destinations.

## Costs and limitations

The route is **Solana USDC to Solana USDC**. Destinations must be your own Cove wallets within one profile. Review the live exchange fee and each minimum rather than relying on a historical example.

Routing changes how payments appear on-chain; it is not a guarantee of anonymity or the absence of provider records. Once payment is sent, it cannot be recalled.

For an external destination, see [Private Withdraw](/funds/private-withdraw).
