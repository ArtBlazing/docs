To send a token without selling it, use **Transfer** on its position. The regular `/withdraw` flow is for stablecoin withdrawals.

## Send to an external wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the holding">
    Open the correct profile and send `/positions`. Expand the token's wallet details if needed, then tap **Transfer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the source">
    Verify **From** or **Account from**. Where a source picker is available, choose the wallet holding the tokens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the destination">
    Select **Send to external wallet** and enter the recipient's address on the token's chain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the amount">
    Use the amount or percentage controls offered. Review the token, chain, source, destination, and any costs before sending.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check completion">
    Follow **Transfer sent**, then **Transfer complete** or **Transfer failed**. Use the explorer link where offered.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Move tokens between your wallets

Choose **Transfer between my accounts**, then:

- **Simple transfer:** one source and one destination.
- **Disperse:** one source and several destinations.
- **Collect:** several sources into one destination.

Select wallets, enter the amount, review the preview, and check each result after sending. These moves keep the token on its existing chain.

The source-wallet picker and profile controls appear only where available. If you change profiles, reopen the transfer from that profile's Positions list.

## If the transfer cannot proceed

**No token in this profile** means you should check the holding profile. **Panel expired** or **Out of date** means reopen the transfer. A token contract may restrict transfers, so a displayed balance does not guarantee the token can be moved.

Paper wallets cannot transfer real tokens. Never use a token's contract address as the recipient wallet.
