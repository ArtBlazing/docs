Cove DCA is a **ladder of limit orders across a market-cap range**. Each target, or “rung,” triggers independently. It is not an hourly, daily, or weekly recurring purchase.

## Create a buy ladder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the token and wallets">
    Paste the token address in the intended profile and check the selected wallets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose DCA and Buying">
    Open **Limit → DCA**, then choose **Buying**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the market-cap range">
    Enter **From mcap** and **To mcap**. You can use shorthand such as **800k** or **1.5m**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the order count and total">
    Choose **2–10 orders** and enter **Total spend** in USD. This is the budget for the whole ladder, not each rung.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review targets and expiry">
    Check the preview, selected wallets, and expiry. The default is **30 days**; the allowed range is 1 minute–30 days.

    If the range crosses the current market cap, review the mixed-ladder warning: some rungs buy dips and others buy breakouts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place and verify">
    Tap **Place N buy orders**. Check the receipt, then open `/orders` to verify the rungs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example: buy in three steps

Assume the current market cap is **1m**. You choose **600k to 900k**, **3 orders**, and **\$300 total**.

| Target | Illustrative allocation |
|---|---|
| 600k | \$100 |
| 750k | \$100 |
| 900k | \$100 |

The targets are evenly spaced. Each rung waits for its own condition; the highest dip target may execute first.

With multiple wallets, **DCA buys always split the total across wallets**, even if ordinary buys use **Per Wallet**. The rung count is **per wallet**: three rungs across two wallets can create six orders. Check the actual preview for allocation and any minimum-size restriction.

## Create a sell ladder

1. Open **Limit → DCA → Selling** on a holding.
2. Enter **From mcap** and **To mcap**.
3. Choose **2–10 orders**.
4. Set **Sell total %**, from 1–100, for the whole ladder.
5. Review targets, wallets, expiry, and any skipped rungs.
6. Tap **Place up to N sell orders** and verify the receipt.

Cove divides the sell allocation into equal absolute slices. A 100% sell across four rungs aims to allocate four equal quarters, not four sequential 25%-of-remaining sells.

Rungs exactly at current market cap and wallets without a holding can be skipped. The receipt can therefore contain fewer orders than the maximum.

## Create by replying to a panel

<Warning>The complete command places the ladder immediately using the selected wallets. Sending it is the confirmation. The numbers below are examples, not recommended trade sizes.</Warning>

Reply to the token's live panel:

```text
/dca B 800k - 1.5m 10 500
```

This means **buy**, range **800k–1.5m**, **10 rungs per wallet**, **\$500 total**.

```text
/dca S 80k - 120k 10 100
```

This means **sell**, range **80k–120k**, **10 rungs**, **100% total**.

Send `/dca` alone for the tutorial. Use **B** or **S**, a range, a count from 2–10, then USD for buys or a holdings percentage for sells.

## Edit, cancel, and follow results

Open `/orders`. Edit a pending rung or cancel it individually. **Cancel DCA group** cancels the ladder's remaining orders; already executed trades are not undone.

All rungs can trigger close together if the market moves through the range. A rung whose target is never met remains pending until expiry. Each fill has trading costs and can fail independently.

For a duplicate-command warning, expired panel, or uncertain partial creation, check existing orders before retrying. See [Manage orders](/features/managing-orders).
