Cove offers two DCA strategies under **Limit → DCA**:

| Mode | How it works | Order count |
|---|---|---|
| **Price ladder** | Places separate limit orders across a required market-cap range. Each rung waits for its own price target. | 2–10 per wallet |
| **Time Interval** | Starts a standing plan that attempts one market order per interval while the market is within your optional range. | 2–20 per wallet |

Choose the mode before entering values. The two modes can produce very different timing and fills.

## Create a price ladder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the token and wallets">
    Paste the token address in the intended profile and check the selected wallets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose DCA and Buying">
    Open **Limit → DCA → Price ladder**, then choose **Buying**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the market-cap range">
    Enter both **Min mcap** and **Max mcap**. You can use shorthand such as **800k** or **1.5m**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the order count and total">
    Choose **2–10 orders** and select one of the three buy amount presets or **custom**. The amount is the total budget for the ladder, not each rung. These are the same amount presets used in the Limit builder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review targets and expiry">
    Check the preview, selected wallets, and expiry. The default is **30 days**; the allowed range is 1 minute–30 days.

    If the range crosses the current market cap, review the mixed-ladder warning: some rungs buy dips and others buy breakouts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place and verify">
    Tap the **Place … buy orders** button. It includes the order count and total spend. Check the receipt, then open `/orders` to verify the rungs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example: buy in three steps

Assume the current market cap is **1m**. You choose **600k to 900k**, **3 orders**, and **\$300 total**.

| Target | Illustrative allocation |
|---|---|
| 600k | \$100 |
| 750k | \$100 |
| 900k | \$100 |

The targets are evenly spaced. Each rung waits for its own condition; the highest dip target may execute first.

With multiple wallets, **DCA buys always split the total across wallets**, even if ordinary buys use **Per Wallet**. The rung count is **per wallet**: three rungs across two wallets can create six orders. Check the actual preview for allocation and any minimum-size restriction.

## Create a sell ladder

1. Open **Limit → DCA → Price ladder → Selling** on a holding.
2. Enter both **Min mcap** and **Max mcap**.
3. Choose **2–10 orders**.
4. Choose a sell amount preset or **custom** for the total percentage to sell, from 1–100, across the ladder.
5. Review targets, wallets, expiry, and any skipped rungs.
6. Tap **Place up to N sell orders** and verify the receipt.

Cove divides the sell allocation into equal absolute slices. A 100% sell across four rungs aims to allocate four equal quarters, not four sequential 25%-of-remaining sells.

Rungs exactly at current market cap and wallets without a holding can be skipped. The receipt can therefore contain fewer orders than the maximum.

## Create a Time Interval plan

1. Open the token in the intended profile and select the wallets, then choose **Limit → DCA → Time Interval** and **Buying** or **Selling**.
2. Set **Order count** to 2–20. With several wallets, the field is **Orders per wallet**; the creation button shows the combined count.
3. Select a **Buy amount** or **Sell amount** preset, or enter a custom total. Cove divides that total across the planned orders. A sell percentage applies to each selected wallet's own holding.
4. Set **Interval**. The picker offers **1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, 4h, 24h**, plus **Custom** from 1 minute to 24 hours. The initial interval is **15m**.
5. Optionally set **Min mcap**, **Max mcap**, both, or neither. Enter **none** to clear a bound. If both are set, Min must be below Max. Without a range, the plan follows the timer alone.
6. Review the preview, expiry, wallet count, per-order size, and the side-specific bound. A buy with Max mcap never pays above that price; a sell with Min mcap never sells below it. Then tap the **Place … orders** button and check `/orders`.

If the price is outside the range when a piece is due, the plan waits. Missed intervals are not piled into a burst; the next eligible evaluation attempts one piece and resumes the spacing. The duration estimate assumes the price remains in range, so the plan can take longer or expire before all pieces run. Each executed piece has its own trading costs; small orders can cost proportionally more.

## Create by replying to a panel

<Warning>The complete command places the ladder immediately using the selected wallets. Sending it is the confirmation. The numbers below are examples, not recommended trade sizes.</Warning>

Reply to the token's live panel:

```text
/dca B 800k - 1.5m 10 500
```

This means **buy**, range **800k–1.5m**, **10 price-ladder rungs per wallet**, **\$500 total**.

```text
/dca S 80k - 120k 10 100
```

This means **sell**, range **80k–120k**, **10 rungs**, **100% total**.

Send `/dca` alone for the tutorial. This reply-command grammar creates a **price ladder**: use **B** or **S**, a range, a count from 2–10, then USD for buys or a holdings percentage for sells. Use the panel to create a Time Interval plan.

## Edit, cancel, and follow results

Open `/orders`. For a price ladder, edit or cancel a pending rung; **Cancel DCA group** cancels its remaining rungs. A Time Interval plan appears as its own plan in the token's order list; open it to inspect progress and cancel the remaining plan. Completed trades are not undone.

Price-ladder rungs can trigger close together if the market moves through the range. A rung whose target is never met remains pending until expiry. Each fill has trading costs and can fail independently.

For a duplicate-command warning, expired panel, or uncertain partial creation, check existing orders before retrying. See [Manage orders](/features/managing-orders).
