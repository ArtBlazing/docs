DCA orders create a ladder of planned orders between two market-cap values.

## Create a ladder

1. Open a token's Buy or Sell panel.
2. Select `Limit` and then the DCA flow.
3. Enter the starting market cap and ending market cap.
4. Enter the number of orders.
5. Enter the total spend for a buy ladder or the total sell amount for a sell ladder.
6. Review the preview and expiry.
7. Select `Place orders`.

The preview lists the generated ladder, amount per order, and expiry.

## Buy and sell DCA

Buy DCA uses a total spend. Sell DCA uses a total sell amount. Active DCA orders appear in the position card and active order views.

Review the generated ladder, per-order amount, and expiry before placing the orders.
