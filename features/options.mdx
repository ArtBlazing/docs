## Trade panel settings

Trade panel settings control how the token trading panel behaves after you paste a contract address or open a token link.

<Frame><img src="/assets/options/trade-panel-settings.png" alt="Trade panel settings in Cove" /></Frame>

| Option | What it controls |
|---|---|
| Buy Presets | Quick buy amounts shown on the buy panel |
| Sell Presets | Quick sell percentages shown on the sell panel |
| Buy Confirm | Requires confirmation before a buy executes |
| Sell Confirm | Requires confirmation before a sell executes |
| Capital at Risk / Profit Taken | Shows capital-at-risk and profit-taken information in the panel |
| Stay on buy after buying | Keeps the panel on the buy side after a buy completes |
| Auto-buy on paste | Automatically starts the buy flow when a token address is pasted |
| Extra links | Shows additional token links in the panel |

### Buy presets

Buy presets are the quick amount buttons shown on the buy panel. Tap a preset in settings to change its value, or reset the full preset set to defaults.

<Frame><img src="/assets/options/buy-presets.png" alt="Buy preset amounts in Cove" /></Frame>

### Degen mode

Degen mode is a fast-execution mode for users who intentionally want fewer protections.

<Warning>
Degen mode forces 100% slippage, turns liquidity protection off, and keeps auto max-buy clamp on. Your stored slippage, liquidity protection, and clamp settings are restored when degen mode is turned off.
</Warning>

## Auto TP/SL

Auto TP/SL automatically creates matching limit orders for future market buys across supported chains. When enabled, every new market buy can attach a take-profit order, a stop-loss order, or both.

<Frame><img src="/assets/options/auto-tp-sl.png" alt="Auto TP SL overview in Cove" /></Frame>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Auto take profit">
    Auto take profit creates a sell order once the token moves up by your selected profit percentage.

    In the example below, Cove sells **50%** of the position when the token is up **100%**.

    <Frame><img src="/assets/options/auto-take-profit.png" alt="Auto take profit settings" /></Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Auto stop loss">
    Auto stop loss creates a sell order once the token moves down by your selected loss percentage.

    In the example below, Cove sells **100%** of the position when the token is down **30%**.

    <Frame><img src="/assets/options/auto-stop-loss.png" alt="Auto stop loss settings" /></Frame>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
Auto TP/SL applies to future market buys. Existing positions are not changed retroactively.
</Note>

## Execution protection

These options help keep execution predictable during volatile markets and unusual liquidity conditions.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Slippage" icon="gauge">
    Slippage is the maximum price movement Cove will tolerate between submission and execution.

    Use presets such as **10%**, **20%**, **40%**, or **80%**, or set a custom value. The custom range is **1% to 100%** with up to 2 decimals.

    <Frame><img src="/assets/options/slippage.png" alt="Slippage settings" /></Frame>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Liquidity protection" icon="shield">
    Liquidity protection blocks buys that would consume too much of the token's reported liquidity pool.

    Cove blocks a trade when your buy is more than roughly **33%** of the pool's reported liquidity. For example, a **\$30** pool blocks buys above **\$10**.

    <Frame><img src="/assets/options/liquidity-protection.png" alt="Liquidity protection settings" /></Frame>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Auto-adjust max buy" icon="arrow-down">
    Auto-adjust max buy reduces your order to the contract's maximum allowed buy amount when you try to buy more than the token allows.

    <Frame><img src="/assets/options/auto-adjust-max-buy.png" alt="Auto-adjust max buy settings" /></Frame>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Gas surplus budget" icon="gas-pump">
    Gas surplus budget lets Cove cover network fees above the sponsored cap up to a per-trade limit from your USDC balance instead of failing the trade.

    Set it to **Off** to always ask before charging surplus. Presets include **\$1**, **\$3**, **\$5**, **\$10**, and custom.

    <Frame><img src="/assets/options/gas-surplus-budget.png" alt="Gas surplus budget settings" /></Frame>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Group link controls

Group link controls define how shared buy and sell links behave when someone opens them from a group, channel, or external post.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Group buys">
    Group buy sets the maximum amount that can execute instantly from a shared group buy link.

    Trades above your limit ask for confirmation first. Set the option to **Off** to disable group buys entirely.

    <Frame><img src="/assets/options/group-buy.png" alt="Group buy settings" /></Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Group sells">
    Group sell enables or disables group sell links.

    When enabled, group sell links can sell the configured percentage of your own position. When disabled, Cove asks before enabling group sells.

    <Frame><img src="/assets/options/group-sell.png" alt="Group sell settings" /></Frame>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Position panel settings

Position panel settings control which quick sell actions appear in the Positions screen and generated position links.

<Frame><img src="/assets/options/position-panel-settings.png" alt="Position panel settings" /></Frame>

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| Sell 100% in Positions | Shows a one-tap full-sell action from the Positions panel |
| Sell 100% Links | Enables full-sell links for position actions |
