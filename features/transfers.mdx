---
title: Transfers & Withdrawals
description: Move your funds securely both on-chain and directly to other Telegram users
---

Cove features a unified USDC architecture, allowing seamless movement of capital across networks and peer-to-peer within the messenger.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Transfers">
    Send funds directly to other Telegram users instantly. It requires zero gas and executes entirely off-chain.

    1. Click the **Transfer** button from the main menu.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/transfer%20menu.png" alt="The Transfer Menu" /></Frame>
    2. Click **Transfer Details**.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/transfer%20to.png" alt="Transfer Target Menu" /></Frame>
    3. Specify the target by entering their exact Telegram `username`.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/transfer%20to%20telegram%20user%20.png" alt="Enter Telegram Username" /></Frame>
    4. Enter the amount to transfer.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/transfer%20to%20tg%20user_enter%20amount.png" alt="Enter Transfer Amount" /></Frame>
    5. Review the generated transfer ticket.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/transfer%20to%20tg%20user_data%20filled.png" alt="Transfer Data Filled" /></Frame>
    6. Click **Confirm**. Cove executes the transaction instantly and outputs a success receipt.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/transfer%20to%20tg%20user_complete.png" alt="P2P Transfer Completed Successfully" /></Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Withdrawals">
    Withdraw your Unified USDC to any external EVM or Solana wallet across our 5 supported chains.

    1. Click the **Withdraw** button from the main menu.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/withdraw%20menu.png" alt="The Withdraw Menu" /></Frame>
    2. Select the destination chain for the withdrawal (e.g., SOL, ETH, BASE). Cove automatically unifies and routes your USDC to the selected network.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/withdraw_select%20chain.png" alt="Selecting the Destination Chain" /></Frame>
    3. Paste the destination wallet address.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/withdraw_enter%20destination%20wallet%20address.png" alt="Entering Destination Address" /></Frame>
    4. Enter the exact withdrawal amount.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/withdraw_enter%20amount%20to%20withdraw.png" alt="Entering Withdrawal Amount" /></Frame>
    5. Review the final withdrawal ticket and network fee.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/withdraw_data%20filled.png" alt="Reviewing Withdrawal Data" /></Frame>
    6. Click **Confirm**. Cove settles the transaction on-chain and outputs the explorer link.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/withdrawal_complete.png" alt="On-chain Withdrawal Execution Complete" /></Frame>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
