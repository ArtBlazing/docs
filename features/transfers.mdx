Send `/transfer` to open **Transfer USDC**. These transfers use **USDC on Solana**, with a minimum of **\$0.01**. Check available funds and any displayed cost before the final send action.

| Your goal | Choose |
|---|---|
| One wallet to another, including another profile | **Simple transfer** |
| One wallet to several of your wallets | **Disperse USDC** |
| Several wallets into one | **Collect USDC** |
| Send to another Cove user | **To Telegram user** |
| Send a token other than USDC | [Transfer from Positions](/funds/token-transfers) |
| Fund wallets using routed payments | [Private Disperse](/funds/private-disperse), if offered |

## Transfer between your wallets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Simple transfer">
    Open `/transfer` → **Simple transfer** and select the paying wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the receiving wallet">
    Pick your destination wallet. The picker can browse another real profile for a cross-profile transfer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the amount and send">
    Enter the amount or percentage. Review source, destination, and total before the final send action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the receipt">
    Look for the completed result and check both wallets. A sent or processing message is not yet a completion.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Send to another Cove user

1. Open `/transfer` → **To Telegram user**.
2. Select your paying wallet and enter the recipient's **@username**.
3. Check that Cove resolves the intended recipient.
4. Enter the amount and review the final send action.
5. Check the receipt.

The recipient must have started Cove and have a Telegram username. Funds arrive in their **primary wallet**, and they receive a **Transfer Received** notification.

## Disperse USDC

1. Choose **Disperse USDC**.
2. Select one paying wallet and your receiving wallets in the same profile.
3. Set the amount using the offered controls.
4. Review the preview for **each destination**, then submit.
5. Check the per-wallet results.

## Collect USDC

1. Choose **Collect USDC** when at least two wallets hold funds.
2. Select the source wallets and one receiving wallet.
3. Review the amounts to collect, then submit.
4. Check the combined result and each source.

Disperse and Collect stay within **one profile** and on the same chain. Use Simple transfer to move between profiles.

## Partial results and errors

A multi-wallet operation can succeed for some destinations and fail for others. Read every ✅/❌ result and explorer link before retrying the missing part.

If you see **User not found**, ask the recipient to start Cove. If a panel expires or the profile changes, reopen `/transfer`. Paper wallets cannot send real funds.
