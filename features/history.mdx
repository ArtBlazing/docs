Open `/positions` → **History** to launch the **Portfolio Mini App**. Its figures cover a token's **lifetime trading history**, which can differ from the current position shown in Telegram.

## Review portfolio history

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open History">
    Check the active profile in Telegram, then open **History** from Positions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find a token">
    Search, select **All / Open / Closed**, or filter by chain and wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a sort order">
    Use **Recent**, **Top PnL**, **Worst PnL**, **Top PnL %**, or **Worst PnL %**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect the result">
    Read the token's lifetime figures, any **Partial** label, and action tags such as Limit, SL, Breakout, or Transfer. Generate a PnL card where offered.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read your PnL

| Term | Plain-language meaning |
|---|---|
| **Realized PnL** | Profit or loss already realized through sells |
| **Unrealized PnL** | Estimated gain or loss on tokens still held |
| **PnL \$** | Realized plus unrealized profit/loss for the displayed scope |
| **PnL %** | That PnL relative to the recorded total cost |
| **Cost basis** | Recorded cost attributed to your holding |
| **Sold** | Proceeds received from selling |
| **Avg Entry** | Average buy price and its market-cap equivalent |
| **Capital at Risk / Profit Taken** | A summarized view of capital still exposed versus proceeds already taken |

Example, before fees: buy **\$100** of a token, sell half for **\$80**, and value the remaining half at **\$70**. The sold half cost \$50, so realized PnL is **\$30**. Unrealized PnL is **\$20**. Combined PnL is **\$50**, or **50%** of the original \$100 cost.

## Current cycle versus lifetime

The bot normally shows the **current holding cycle**, beginning when you entered from a zero holding. Portfolio history uses **lifetime** data across cycles.

Enable **Total PnL per token** in [Trade panel settings](/features/settings/trade-panel) for lifetime headlines on the token panel. Check that both views use the same profile, wallets, and data coverage before comparing.

## PnL Calendar

Open the **Calendar** tab for daily **Net** PnL, **Current Streak**, **Best Streak**, and **W/L**.

Treat these as product performance summaries, not a complete financial record. Use [CSV export](/features/export) for row-level history. If a day or streak looks different from your calculations, check the period and ask support to clarify the calendar's day boundary.

## Missing or partial figures

Transferred or externally acquired tokens can lack cost history. An earlier holding period may be excluded if it could not be measured. The app shows **Partial** or **Unavailable** instead of a complete figure.

Native/wrapped-native PnL may be unavailable. A missing PnL does not prove funds are missing.

For large discrepancies, check profile, filters, current cycle versus lifetime, and incomplete wallet coverage, then contact [Support](/features/support) with the relevant receipt.
