Cove features an integrated support architecture. You resolve issues and submit features entirely within the app. 

Click **Contact** from the main menu to launch the hub.

<Frame><img src="/assets/contact.png" alt="The Support Portal Menu" /></Frame>

## Portal Management
<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Support">
    Open a direct, private support ticket with the Cove engineering team if you encounter an execution bug or account synchronization issue.

    1. Select **Create Ticket** from the Support portal.
    2. Choose the specific category of your issue (e.g., Transactions, Deposit, Import).
       <Frame><img src="/assets/contact_get%20help.png" alt="Selecting support category" /></Frame>
    3. Cove opens a secure chat channel. Describe your issue in detail. Add any relevant transaction hashes.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/contact_get%20help_sent_adding%20details.png" alt="Adding details to the support ticket" /></Frame>
    4. Cove generates your ticket ID.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/support_after%20tickets%20created.png" alt="Support Ticket Created Confirmation" /></Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Feedback">
    Submit specific UI/UX feature requests or generic terminal feedback directly to our product managers.

    1. Select **Send Feedback** from the contact portal matrix.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/contact_send%20feedback.png" alt="Selecting send feedback" /></Frame>
    2. Cove opens a chat prompt. Detail your request.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/contact_send%20feedback_sent_add%20details.png" alt="Typing feedback into chat" /></Frame>
    3. Hit send. Cove securely transmits your feedback payload and outputs a delivery receipt.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/contact_send%20feedback_sent.png" alt="Feedback successfully sent receipt" /></Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Tickets">
    Track the real-time resolution status of your created support tickets and communicate asynchronously with agents via our dedicated **My Tickets** terminal.

    1. Open the **My Tickets** menu from the support portal. Here you view a ledger of all open issues.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/support_my%20tickets.png" alt="Viewing active support tickets list" /></Frame>
    2. Click on any specific active ticket to drill down. You can view the full conversational history, timestamp, and current resolution status.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/support_my%20tickets_ticket%20example.png" alt="Viewing a specific support ticket's details" /></Frame>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
