Cove Buy turns every token scan in your group/channel into a 1-click buy experience (no copy-paste, no switching apps). Cove Buy is a Telegram tool that adds Quick Buy buttons under scanned tokens so your members can enter faster — and you can earn from the activity in your community.

## For Group Owners
- 💸 Earn from buys
- 💸 Earn from sells too
- 🔗 Add custom ref links 
- ⬆ Increase Engagement

## For Group Members
- 🚀 Faster entries
- 📊 All-in-one token data
- 🏆 Group Leaderboard
- 🧼 Cleaner trading UX for groups

---

## How Cove Buy Works

1. Add [@cove_buy_bot](https://t.me/cove_buy_bot) to your group/channel and give admin rights.
2. Configure your Group Settings.
3. Members see Quick Buy buttons under scanned tokens → they tap → trade executes through Cove.

Cove Buy is a user-facing tool. Your members don’t need to understand setup — they just tap Buy. All referral fees go directly to your Cove account.

## FAQ

**"I already have other scanners in my group — why do I need Cove Buy?"**
Cove Buy routes trades through Cove, and referral fees go directly to your account. Let your users buy via their favourite trading bot, ref fees will still go to your account. Make people trade without leaving your group. Organize contests, reward the best traders and scanners with your referral rewards.

**"Can I buy tokens using Cove Buy without a Cove account?"**
To buy tokens via Cove Buy, a user needs a Cove Bot account (it takes ~2 clicks to create).
