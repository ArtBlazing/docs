Cove supports **English** and **中文**. English is the documented default.

## Change language

1. Send `/language`, or open **Settings → Language**.
2. Choose **English** or **中文**.
3. Reopen the panel you want to use to see its localized labels.

Language is shared across your account rather than set separately for each profile. Mini Apps use the account locale. The guides here quote the English labels.

Telegram's own theme controls the bot appearance; Cove has no separate documented bot theme switch.

Chinese token-name translation, where offered, is a token-panel feature and is separate from changing the whole interface language.
