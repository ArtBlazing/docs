Auto TP/SL can create take-profit, stop-loss, and trailing-stop orders after eligible buys in the active profile. **Market buys** and **Limit & DCA** have separate templates and on/off states. Review both tabs before relying on an exit rule.

Targets use the purchase's entry, regardless of the **Manual TP/SL basis** setting.

## Configure a ladder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Auto TP/SL">
    Choose **Settings → Trade panel settings → Auto TP/SL**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the buy mode">
    Open **Market buys** for immediate buys or **Limit & DCA** for a filled limit buy, DCA rung, or breakout buy. A green indicator on the other tab means it also has an armed rule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the side">
    Open **Auto take profit**, **Auto stop loss**, or **Auto trailing stop**. Each is configured separately for the selected buy mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a level">
    For Auto TP or SL, choose a trigger percentage and **Sell %** for each level. Take-profit gains allow **1–100,000%**; stop-loss losses allow **1–99%**; sell size allows **1–100%**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose sell sizing">
    Check **Sell size → % of entry** or **% of remaining**. Read the examples below before adding several levels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add levels where available">
    Use **Add level** or **Delete**. Review the total allocation and estimated return summary. Enable the intended side.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify after your next buy">
    Open `/orders` after an eligible fill to confirm the expected exits were created. A Price ladder DCA arms its exit template once on the first rung, using that rung's fill price as the reference.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>Accounts with multi-level controls can configure up to **20 TP levels and 20 SL levels**. If Add level is absent, use the single level available in your account.</Note>

## Choose how a take-profit sells

In **Auto take profit → Sell style**, choose **Market** or **Dynamic**. In thin liquidity, Dynamic can avoid a whole-level dump that pushes the price down: Cove sells smaller amounts when needed **at its target price or better** and waits with the remainder until the price allows. It sizes each trade from live quotes; there is no pieces or retry-interval setting. Market attempts to sell the entire level at once at the available market quote.

Dynamic can be useful when a whole-position sale would move a thin market. It may take several fills and can remain pending if the target price cannot be met. The style belongs to take-profit levels, not stop-loss levels. Manual take-profit steps also offer Market and Dynamic in the Limit builder; see [Take-profit and stop-loss](/features/take-profit-stop-loss).

## Set an automatic trailing stop

In the selected buy-mode tab, open **Auto trailing stop**. Set a **Trail %** and **Sell %**, then enable it. Its reference starts at the buy fill, follows the highest price reached afterward, and attempts a sale after the selected drop from that peak. It always sells a percentage of what you still hold at that moment.

A fixed Auto SL and Auto trailing stop may both be armed. Whichever fires first sells; the other is superseded. Check the active orders after a fill.

## Understand sell size

The default basis is **% of remaining**.

| Basis | Two levels set to sell 50% each |
|---|---|
| **% of entry** | Each allocates half of the position used to size the ladder; the two levels allocate 100% |
| **% of remaining** | First sells half; second sells half of what remains, leaving 25% of the original quantity |

Entry-based allocations cannot exceed 100%. The product may show **100% of entry allocated** when you need to reduce a level before adding another. This sell-size basis also affects manual multi-step limit sells.

The **Total sell** and **Est. return on sold tokens** summary assumes fills at targets, nearest first, before fees/slippage, and excludes unsold tokens. It is not a forecast.

## Buying more and existing orders

A later buy can re-anchor Auto TP/SL targets to the updated average entry. Cove avoids stacking another set when active sell orders already exist for the token.

Some accounts offer live-position sizing for entry-based allocations. Review the actual order sizes after additional buys or manual sells; do not assume existing quantities rebalance automatically unless that behavior is confirmed for your account.

USDC, native-token, and automatic bridge operations do not create these exits.

## Disable or change the rules

Turn **Auto TP**, **Auto SL**, or **Auto trailing stop** off in each applicable buy-mode tab to stop future creation for that side. Edit levels for future setup, then separately inspect active orders in `/orders`. Do not assume changing the rule cancels protection already placed.

## Missing or failed protection

**Auto TP/SL Incomplete** means at least one expected order was not created. Add the missing order manually and verify it.

A stop blocked by excess network cost may be **no longer armed**. Set an appropriate [Gas surplus budget](/features/settings/guardrails#gas-surplus-budget) if desired, then recreate it. Increasing the budget alone does not restore the stop.
