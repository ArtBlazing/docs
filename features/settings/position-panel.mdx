Position panel settings control quick actions shown next to positions.

<Frame><img src="/assets/current-ui/position-panel-settings.png" alt="Current Position panel settings with full-sell controls" /></Frame>

| Setting | What it changes |
|---|---|
| Sell 100% in Positions | Shows a full-sell action inside the Positions panel |
| Sell 100% Links | Enables full-sell links for position actions |

An explicit full-sell action opens the Sell flow. Verify the selected account or wallets before confirming a full sell.
