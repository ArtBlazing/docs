## Slippage

Slippage is the price movement you allow while a trade executes. It differs from **price impact**, which is how much the trade itself changes the market price.

1. Send `/slippage`, use **change** on a token panel, or open **Guardrails → Slippage**.
2. Choose **10 / 20 / 40 / 80%**, or **Custom**.
3. Enter a value from **1–100%** and check the updated slippage line.

New-user slippage starts at **15%**; existing settings may differ. Values above 20% show a warning. Raising tolerance can permit a worse fill, not just increase success.

## Guardrail reference

| Setting | Default | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Auto-adjust max buy** | On | Reduces a buy to fit the token's purchase cap; receipt shows actual versus requested spend |
| **Liquidity protection** | On | Blocks buys that are too large relative to reported pool liquidity |
| **Scam warning** | On | Requests confirmation for a 🚨-flagged token on the panel and blocks flagged panel-less buys |
| **High price-impact confirmation** | On | Adds a review for high-impact buys and sells |
| **Degen mode** | Off | Applies 100% slippage, disables liquidity protection, and enables max-buy adjustment |
| **Gas surplus budget** | Off | Allows additional network cost within your chosen limit |

## Scam warnings

If the panel shows **Scam warning**, select **Find out why** to read the concern. **Cancel** leaves the buy. **Buy anyway** accepts the flagged trade; **Disable scam warning** changes the standing profile setting.

With Scam warning on, flagged buys from **auto-buy on paste**, **quick-buy**, and **group links** are refused when no panel confirmation is available. Open the token manually to inspect it.

A clean or pending scan is not a guarantee that a token can be safely bought or sold.

## Price-impact confirmations

Cove may request confirmation when estimated impact reaches **50%**, or when it cannot verify the impact. The prompt shows what you spend and expect to receive. Sells are covered too.

- Turning the setting off skips only the **50–90%** confirmation band.
- **Extreme impact at 90% or above** and unknown impact still require review.
- If a refreshed quote is materially worse, **Quote changed** asks you to review again.

A one-time **Buy anyway / Sell anyway** on an eligible single-wallet market refusal applies to that fresh attempt; it does not change saved settings. This option is not offered for Paper or multi-wallet market refusals.

## Degen mode

Toggle **Degen mode** in Guardrails or the trading panel. It changes the three settings listed above while active. Turning it off restores saved values; editing slippage, liquidity protection, or max-buy adjustment exits Degen mode.

Scam warning and high-impact confirmation remain independent.

<Warning>Degen mode permits very large price movement and removes liquidity protection. It does not make a restricted token sellable or guarantee a fill.</Warning>

## Gas surplus budget

1. Open **Guardrails → Gas surplus budget**.
2. Choose **Off**, **\$1**, **\$3**, **\$5**, **\$10**, or **Custom**.
3. For Custom, enter **\$0–\$50**.
4. Check the saved amount.

With the budget off, extra network costs normally require approval. With a budget, eligible overages up to that amount can be charged automatically, subject to available USDC.

**Auto-pay gas up to** on a failure message sets this future budget. It does not complete the current trade or recreate a failed stop.
