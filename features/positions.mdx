Cove's Positions panel gives you a compact portfolio view across your selected accounts. Use it to monitor token value, PnL, account balances, and position-level actions without leaving Telegram.

Click the **Positions** button on the main menu to open your portfolio.

<Frame><img src="/assets/positions-new/positions-panel.png" alt="Cove Positions panel" /></Frame>

## What you see

Each token row shows the chain, token name, market cap, PnL, position worth, and the account that holds it.

## Panel actions

| Action | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Refresh | Reload the latest balances and PnL |
| Positions Mini App | Open the expanded position experience |
| Filters | Choose which accounts or position groups are visible |
| Sort by Worth | Sort positions by current value |
| History | Review past position activity |
| Hide positions | Hide positions you do not want in the main list |
| Sell 100% | Sell an entire position when enabled in Options |
| Move | Move a position between accounts or destinations |

## Hiding positions

Use **Hide positions** to keep dust, inactive, or low-priority positions out of your main portfolio view.

<Frame><img src="/assets/positions-new/hide-positions.png" alt="Hide positions menu" /></Frame>

From the hide menu:

1. Click **Hide** next to any visible position.
2. Use **Show hidden** to switch into hidden-position view.
3. Click **Unhide** to return a hidden position to the main list.

<Frame><img src="/assets/positions-new/show-hidden.png" alt="Showing hidden positions" /></Frame>

<Note>
Hidden positions are not sold or moved. They are only removed from the default view until you unhide them.
</Note>

## Options that affect Positions

The Positions panel respects your position panel settings from **Options**.

<CardGroup cols={2}>

  <Card title="Sell 100% Links" icon="link">
