Cove centralizes your entire portfolio into a single, cohesive dashboard. You track performance in real-time without switching between block explorers.

Click the **Positions** button on the main menu to open your portfolio.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/positions%20menu.png" alt="Main Positions Dashboard" />
</Frame>

The dashboard displays:
1. Total portfolio value.
2. Individual token balances.
3. Unrealized PnL (Profit and Loss).
4. Current token market cap.

## Advanced Filtering

When managing massive portfolios across 5 chains and multiple wallets, you need precise filtering to locate specific assets. 

1. Click the **Filters** menu at the top of the Positions tab.
   <Frame><img src="/assets/positions_filters%20menu.png" alt="Opening Advanced Filters" /></Frame>

You filter your active positions using three main categories:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="By Account">
    Isolate positions held in specific generated wallets.
    
    Select the checkboxes next to the accounts you wish to view. Cove dynamically updates the list.
    <Frame><img src="/assets/positions_filters_by%20accounts.png" alt="Filtering positions by Account" /></Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="By Chain">
    Filter tokens residing on specific blockchains.

    Cove displays all 5 chains natively. 
    <Frame><img src="/assets/positions_filters_by%20chain.png" alt="Filtering positions by Chain" /></Frame>
    
    Deselect specific chains (e.g., turning off BNB) to hide those assets from the main view.
    <Frame><img src="/assets/positions_filters_by%20chain_deselect%20chains.png" alt="Deselecting BNB chain filter" /></Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Other Metrics">
    Sort and filter the list based on performance and size.

    Sort by PnL (Highest/Lowest) or sort by Value (Highest/Lowest) to identify your best and worst performing assets instantly.
    <Frame><img src="/assets/positions_filters_others.png" alt="Sorting positions by Value and PnL" /></Frame>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
