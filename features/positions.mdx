Open `Positions` from the home screen to view holdings across your selected accounts.

<Frame><img src="/assets/current-ui/positions-overview.png" alt="Current Cove Positions panel with position values" /></Frame>

## Position view

Each row can show the token, chain, market cap, PnL, worth, and account context. The panel also exposes total balance and position value.

## Filter and sort

The current panel includes:

- Sort by Worth.
- Sort by Age.
- Account filters.
- Chain filters.
- Other filters shown in the filter menu.
- Pagination.
- History.
- Refresh.

<Frame><img src="/assets/current-ui/positions-sort-by-age.png" alt="Current Positions controls after switching the sort order to Age" /></Frame>

## Hide and show positions

Use `Hide positions` to remove a position from the default list. Use `Show hidden` to view hidden positions, then unhide a position when you want it back in the main list.

<Frame><img src="/assets/current-ui/positions-hide.png" alt="Current Hide positions view" /></Frame>

<Frame><img src="/assets/current-ui/positions-show-hidden.png" alt="Current Show hidden positions view" /></Frame>

Hiding a position does not sell or move it.

## Position actions

Depending on the settings and current position, the row can expose:

- Sell 100%.
- Sell 100% across selected wallets when the position spans selected accounts.
- Move.
- View detailed.
- Generate PnL.
- Open the Positions Mini App.

`Sell 100%` and `Sell 100% Links` are controlled from Settings, Position panel settings. Confirm the selected accounts and final amount before a full sell.

See [PnL Cards](/features/pnl-cards) for the generated image workflow.
