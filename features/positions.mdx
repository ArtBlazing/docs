Send `/positions` or choose **Positions**. The list shows holdings in the **active profile**, grouped by token and chain and aggregated across its wallets.

## Read a position

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Ticker | Tap to open the token's trading panel |
| **mc** | Current market cap |
| **PnL % / PnL \$** | Reported profit or loss for the displayed scope |
| **Worth** | Estimated current value |
| **Supply %** | Holding as a share of token supply |
| Wallet count / **View detailed** | Expand the wallets that hold the token |

The footer shows spendable balance, positions value, any parked funds, and the profile you are viewing.

## Open or act on a holding

1. Check the profile beside **Viewing**; use **Change profile** if needed.
2. Tap the token to buy more, sell, or [create an order](/features/orders-overview).
3. Expand **View detailed** for wallet-specific values and actions.
4. Use **Transfer** to [send the token without selling](/funds/token-transfers).
5. Use **Generate PnL** for a [share card](/features/pnl-cards).

**Sell 100%** links are shown only if enabled. The Positions **Sell confirmation** setting is on by default. A link sells the holding in its displayed scope, so check whether you are in an aggregate or wallet detail.

## Filter, sort, and hide

Open **Filters** for chains, wallets, dust, liquidity, suspicious tokens, and native/wrapped tokens. **Sort** offers Worth, Age, and PnL. See [Position filters](/features/settings/position-panel).

**Hide** removes a token from the visible list. **View hidden** or the Mini App's **Hidden** list lets you **Unhide** it. Hiding does not sell it.

## Understand incomplete numbers

A token received from outside Cove may lack a reliable cost basis. A row can disclose **tracked / untracked** or **covered / total wallets**. Treat its PnL as partial, even if a balance is visible.

**Unavailable** does not mean zero profit or zero holdings. Native and wrapped-native tokens may show unavailable PnL.

Use [History and PnL](/features/history) for the difference between current-cycle and lifetime figures.

## More ways to view and exit

- **Positions Mini App:** a visual holdings interface.
- **History:** lifetime portfolio and PnL Calendar.
- **SWITCH TO BULK SELL:** [sell selected positions in one run](/features/bulk-sell).
- **Refresh:** update the list.

If the list is empty, check profile, wallet/chain filters, and hidden positions before assuming a token is gone.

## Open the visual positions view

Tap **Positions Mini App** to browse the same portfolio visually. Check the profile, then select a token for its details. See [Use the Mini Apps](/essentials/mini-apps).
