A **take-profit** attempts to sell after a rise. A **stop-loss** attempts to sell after a fall. In the current **Limit → Selling** builder, the direction of each trigger determines which one you create; there is no separate global Stop loss / Take profit switch.

For the exact values accepted by **custom**, see [Custom trigger examples](/features/limit-orders#enter-a-custom-trigger).

You need a holding in the selected wallets.

## Choose the percentage reference first

Open **Settings → Trade panel settings → Manual TP/SL basis**.

| Basis | A percentage starts from… |
|---|---|
| **Market price** — default | The market when you place the order |
| **Entry price** | Each wallet's average entry when you place the order |

For illustration, you bought at **1m** market cap and the market is now **1.5m**. A **20% down** target is **1.2m** with Market price or **0.8m** with Entry price.

This reference is captured when you place a manual order; later buys do not move it. With multiple wallets, entry-based targets can differ. If entry is unavailable, Cove falls back to market and tells you.

## Use breakeven

In the **custom trigger** input, enter **be** or **br** to use your entry market cap. Set the sell size, review, and create the stop. An entry value must be available and the target must be valid.

Breakeven describes the trigger, not guaranteed net recovery after fees, slippage, and price movement.

## Multiple levels and allocation

Add up to three manual steps. You can mix upward take-profit and downward stop-loss triggers in one setup; its button then says **Create orders**. Review each step's direction, amount, and sell style. The **Sell size** basis controls whether percentages allocate from entry or from what remains. See [Auto TP/SL sell sizing](/features/settings/auto-tp-sl#understand-sell-size).

## Market or Dynamic take-profit

In thin liquidity, selling an entire position at once can push the price down. **Dynamic** take-profit tries a smoother exit: Cove sells smaller amounts when needed, at the target price or better, and waits with the remainder until the price allows. The remainder may never fill before expiry. Cove chooses piece sizes from live quotes; you do not set a piece count or retry interval. **Market** instead tries to sell the whole take-profit allocation at the available quote when the target fires.

The Dynamic option appears only for take-profit steps. Stop-loss steps remain Market. You can also select Dynamic for future automatic take-profit levels in [Auto TP/SL](/features/settings/auto-tp-sl#choose-how-a-take-profit-sells).

## Follow the outcome

Default expiry is **30 days**. A brief price wick can trigger an order even if the market has already moved back by execution time. High impact or execution errors can prevent a fill.

Check [Manage orders](/features/managing-orders) for status, edits, and cancellation. Use [Trailing stop](/features/trailing-stop) for a moving peak-based exit, or [Auto TP/SL](/features/settings/auto-tp-sl) for future buys.
