A Cove limit order waits for a market-cap target or percentage move, then attempts a market trade with your selected wallets and applicable settings. The **Limit** builder has separate trigger and amount presets for buys and sells.

Use a **dip buy** below the current market or a **breakout buy** above it. You enter the amount to spend separately from the trigger.

## Buy a dip

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the token and choose wallets">
    Paste the contract address. Check the chain, active profile, selected wallets, and available USDC.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the limit builder">
    Tap **Limit** and choose **Buying** on the **Limit** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a trigger">
    Tap one of the seven trigger presets, or **custom** to enter a market cap, a signed percentage, or a multiple such as **2x**. The arrow on each preset shows whether the target is above or below the reference. The label above the presets shows the reference used for the preview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the target">
    For a dip, choose a downward preset or enter a market cap below the current cap, such as **800k**. A custom percentage such as **-20%** also sets a downward target.

    Accepted market-cap shorthand includes **45k** and **12.5M**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set spending and expiry">
    Tap one of the three USD amount presets or **custom**. Review **Expiry**; the default is **30 days** and the allowed range is 1 minute–30 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place and verify">
    Review the preview line for every step, spend, wallets, and expiry. Tap the creation button and open `/orders` to verify the new order in the correct profile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example

Assume a token's current market cap is **\$1 million**. You want to buy **\$100** if it falls to **\$800,000**.

Choose **custom → 800k** for the trigger and **custom → $100** for the amount, then review and place the order. An equivalent **-20%** target uses the market reference at placement; a fixed **800k** remains that specific target.

This is an illustrative setup. The actual fill depends on the quote and liquidity when the order executes.

## Buy a breakout

Use the same flow with an upward trigger preset or a custom target above the current market cap, such as **+10%** or **2x**. Review the direction and target preview before creating the order.

## Enter a custom trigger

Tap **custom** under **Trigger** to see the formats Cove accepts. The sell prompt groups examples by how Cove will interpret them:

| Enter | Cove reads it as |
|---|---|
| **200k**, **2m**, or **14.5K** | A specific market-cap target |
| **100%**, **+100%**, or **2x** | A take-profit trigger above the reference |
| **-20** or **-20%** | A stop-loss trigger below the reference |

The trigger sets **when** an order can run. Choose the buy or sell amount separately. Check the reference shown on the panel, the resulting target, and the order direction before creating it. On a sell, an unsigned **100%** is a positive trigger; it does not mean “sell 100%” until you select that in **Sell amount**.

## Edit limit presets

Open **Settings → Trade panel settings → Buttons and controls → Limit preset amounts**. Choose **Limit buy presets** or **Limit sell presets**, then switch between **Triggers** and **Buy amounts / Sell amounts**. Each side has seven trigger slots and three amount slots. Tap a slot to edit it; **Reset defaults** restores the selected list. These presets belong to the active profile.

The default buy triggers are **+20%, +50%, +75%, +100%, -20%, -50%, -75%**, with **$200 / $500 / $1,000** amounts. The default sell triggers are **1.5x, 2x, 5x, 10x, -20%, -50%, -75%**, with **20 / 50 / 100%** amounts. Your saved choices may differ. A trigger preset can point up or down on either side.

## More than one target

Where the builder offers **Add step**, you can configure up to **three steps**. Check each target and spend before placing the group. For an evenly spaced range of 2–10 targets, use [DCA](/features/dca-orders).

## Why is the order still open?

The target may not have been reached. A high-impact refusal can also leave the order active and waiting. A failed or expired order is different and may require a new order.

Edit or cancel pending orders in [Manage orders](/features/managing-orders). To automate selling, use [Take-profit and stop-loss](/features/take-profit-stop-loss).
