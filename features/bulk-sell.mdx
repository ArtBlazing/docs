Bulk sell attempts to sell **100% of each selected token** across the active profile's wallets that hold it. It is available in the Telegram Positions list.

## Select and sell

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open bulk-sell mode">
    Send `/positions`, confirm the profile, and tap **SWITCH TO BULK SELL**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a worth filter if useful">
    Choose **Worth filter** and a value such as **\$50**, **\$100**, **\$500**, or **\$x**. **No filter** removes it.

    Changing the worth filter clears the current selection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select positions">
    Use **Select** on individual rows or **Select current page positions**. Tokens marked **❌** are selected for selling.

    **Select all pages positions** requires a worth filter and selects up to the 100-position cap.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the full list">
    Tap **Bulk sell N tokens**. Read **Confirm bulk sell**, including every page of the list and displayed worth.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and follow results">
    Tap **Confirm bulk sell**. Follow the single progress receipt until complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understand selection and results

The maximum is **100 positions per run**. If more match the worth filter, Cove selects the largest matching positions within the cap. Read the selection summary rather than assuming every holding is included.

The receipt reports **Sold X of Y positions** and total USDC received. **Partly sold** means some wallets still hold the token.

<Warning>Displayed worth is an estimate. Low liquidity, price impact, token restrictions, or failed sells can change the amount received and leave positions behind.</Warning>

## If you need to retry

Check the completed results in `/positions` first. Then select only the remaining holdings you intend to sell.

For **Selection or filter changed**, review the current list again. For **Selection expired**, reopen Positions. If another sell is already processing, wait for its receipt before starting another run.
