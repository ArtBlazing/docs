Invite fellow traders to Cove and earn a continuous percentage of their execution fees. The referral system operates natively within the Telegram interface.

## Accessing Your Referral Link

Click **Refer & Earn** from the main menu. Cove opens a panel displaying your unique referral URL and your generated tracking statistics (such as your total number of active referrals).
