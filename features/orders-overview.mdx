Automated orders wait for a condition, then attempt a trade. In Cove, targets use **market capitalization** or a **percentage move**, not an absolute token-price field.

## Which order do I need?

| I want to… | Use |
|---|---|
| Buy below today's market cap | [Limit buy on a dip](/features/limit-orders) |
| Buy if the token rises to a target | [Breakout buy](/features/limit-orders#buy-a-breakout) |
| Sell after a gain | [Take-profit](/features/take-profit-stop-loss) |
| Sell after a fall | [Stop-loss](/features/take-profit-stop-loss) |
| Sell after a fall from a new peak | [Trailing stop](/features/trailing-stop) |
| Spread buys or sells over market-cap targets | [DCA Price ladder](/features/dca-orders#create-a-price-ladder) |
| Buy or sell in timed pieces, with optional market-cap bounds | [DCA Time Interval](/features/dca-orders#create-a-time-interval-plan) |
| Sell a take-profit level in smaller fills when the target price allows | [Dynamic take-profit](/features/take-profit-stop-loss#market-or-dynamic-take-profit) |
| Add protective orders after future buys | [Auto TP/SL](/features/settings/auto-tp-sl) |
| Receive a notification without trading | [Price alert](/features/price-alerts) |

## Understand triggers and execution

For example, a **\$1 million market-cap target** means the order waits for the token's market cap to reach that level. It is not a request to trade \$1 million.

Once triggered, an order executes at the available market quote with applicable settings. A target does **not** guarantee the fill price or proceeds. Fixed take-profit and stop-loss orders can also react to a brief price wick and execute after the price has moved again.

Trigger checks are typically around tens of seconds; network and market conditions can delay execution. Thin liquidity, slippage, fees, or token restrictions can prevent a fill.

## Shared controls

- Open a token → **Limit**, then choose **Limit**, **DCA**, or sell-side **Trailing SL**. DCA offers **Price ladder** and **Time Interval**.
- Buy orders use **USD spend**; sell orders use a **percentage of the holding**.
- Default expiry is **30 days**, with a minimum of **1 minute** and maximum of **30 days**.
- Native and launchpad panels do not offer these automated-order controls.
- Manage pending orders in `/orders` within their profile.

<Note>
Manual alerts expire after 7 days by default; automatic alerts after 5 days. Alert expiry is different from order expiry.
</Note>

## Before placing several orders

Check combined spending and sell allocation, selected wallets, the manual TP/SL basis, and existing active orders. Do not assume every pending order has funds reserved: keep sufficient spendable balance and review the actual panel.

In **Price ladder**, DCA orders are independent market-cap triggers, and several rungs can trigger close together. In **Time Interval**, one piece is attempted per interval when the optional price range permits it. An active plan can remain waiting for price or expire before all pieces fill.

After placement, open [Manage orders](/features/managing-orders) to verify each order. Orders keep running when you close Telegram, switch profiles, or archive a profile.
