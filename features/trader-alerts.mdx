Send `/track` to open **Trader Tracker** if the feature is available on your account. It can send Telegram alerts when a trader you follow on **FOMO** or **Pump.fun** buys or sells a token, or publishes a thesis. Receiving an alert does not place a trade.

<Note>Trader Tracker may be temporarily unavailable. If `/track` says so, your existing subscriptions are kept, but new alerts are not delivered while the feature is off.</Note>

## Add a trader

1. Send `/track` and tap **Add new Trader**.
2. Enter a wallet address, an `@handle`, or a supported FOMO or Pump.fun profile link. A profile link searches that app; a handle searches both.
3. If the trader appears on both apps, choose **Fomo**, **Pump.fun**, or **Both**. If no match is found, try a different handle or browse **Leaderboard**.
4. Open **Manage** to review the subscription and its alert filters.

The home screen shows your tracked traders and whether each is live or paused. **Pause All** and **Resume All** affect delivery for your list; they do not change your holdings or orders.

## Choose what you receive

Each trader has separate settings. In **Manage**, set **Theses** to Off, All, First on Token, or My Holdings. Set **Trades** to buys, first buys only, sells, or off. **Min buy** and **Token Filters** can narrow alerts by market cap, liquidity, volume, holders, top-holder share, and token age. Changes save as you make them; there is no separate Save button.

Use **Pause** to stop that trader's alerts without deleting the subscription. **Delete** removes it after confirmation. The trader's public wallets and source app are shown for identification; check them before changing filters.

## Read an alert before acting

An alert can show the trader, token, event, market data, and action buttons. The **Buy / Limit** choice changes how an eligible buy action is placed; switching the choice alone does not trade. Tapping an amount may submit a trade, so check the token, selected wallets, mode, and amount first. An alert is information about someone else's activity, not a promise that you will get their price.
