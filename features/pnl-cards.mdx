A PnL card is an image of a token position's performance. You can generate one from the token panel or a full-sell receipt.

## Create a card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the right position">
    In the intended profile, open the token or its 100%-sell receipt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Generate PnL">
    Tap **Generate PnL**. Portfolio and position Mini Apps can also offer this action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a format">
    Select **Regular PnL** for the full card, or **Only % gain** to omit dollar figures. **Cancel** leaves the flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review before sharing">
    Cove sends the image to your chat. Check the token, numbers, and any partial-coverage caption before forwarding or downloading it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Which format should I use?

| Format | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Regular PnL** | Percentage plus invested, position, and PnL amounts |
| **Only % gain** | Percentage without dollar-size figures |

Multi-wallet cards may disclose that not every wallet has complete PnL data. Read [PnL scope](/features/history) when a card differs from another view.

Custom backgrounds are not a documented self-service upload feature. Use the available designs or contact support about your account's options.
