Cove is designed to be completely gasless. It requires zero bridging. It supports an instant, unified USDC balance across MegaETH, ETH, SOL, BNB, and BASE.

<Stepper steps={[{label: 'Start'}, {label: 'Fund'}, {label: 'Trade'}]} currentStep={1} />

## 1. Start the Bot

Open [Cove Bot](https://t.me/cove_trading_bot) in Telegram. Click **Start**. The dashboard opens instantly.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/cove_start.png" alt="Cove Start Screen" />
</Frame>

The home screen shows your balance, active positions, and main action menus.

## 2. Make a Deposit

You must fund your account before trading. Cove uses a Unified USDC Balance. You do not need specific gas tokens for different chains. Deposit funds on any chain. Trade on any other chain instantly.

1. Click **Deposit** on the main menu.
2. Select the chain you want to deposit from.
3. Send any token to the generated secure deposit address. Cove automatically swaps it into USDC.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/deposit.png" alt="Deposit menu" />
</Frame>

## 3. Execute a Trade

Send any token contract address (CA) directly to the bot to summon the trading panel. You can find CAs on platforms like Dexscreener or in official project channels. Cove fetches the chart and liquidity data instantly.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/sendCA%20to%20start%20trading.png" alt="Prompt to send Contract Address" />
</Frame>

The trading panel appears. Click your configured preset button (e.g., $50) to instantly fill the order. You are now managing an active position. Monitor it in the **Positions** tab.
