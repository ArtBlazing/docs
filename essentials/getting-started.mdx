Start the bot, add USDC on Solana, buy a token, and find it in your positions. Cove creates your first wallet for you; you do not need to create or import another one to get started.

To learn with virtual money first, use [Paper trading](/essentials/paper-trading).

<Note>Screenshots use different example accounts and tokens. Follow the steps in your own account and always copy your own deposit address. The amounts and settings shown are examples, not recommendations. Select an image to enlarge it.</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start Cove">
    Open the [Cove Trading](https://t.me/cove_trading_bot) bot in Telegram and tap **Start**, or send `/start`. Complete the age confirmation if shown.

    Cove creates your **Main** profile and a wallet. The main menu gives you access to **Deposit**, **Positions**, and **Settings**.

    {/* Screenshot: start-home.png | Cove's main menu with the active profile, balance, positions, and navigation buttons. */}
    <div className="cove-guide-figure">
      <Frame caption="Cove's main menu with the active profile, balance, positions, and navigation buttons.">
        <img src="/assets/guide-screenshots/start-home.png" alt="Cove's main menu with the active profile, balance, positions, and navigation buttons." width={752} height={698} className="cove-guide-screenshot" />
      </Frame>
    </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deposit USDC on Solana">
    Tap **Deposit** or send `/deposit`. Copy your wallet's address under **Deposit SOL**.

    In the wallet or exchange you are sending from, select **USDC** and the **Solana** network, then use that address. **Deposit SOL** labels the network address; for this guide, the asset you send is **USDC**.

    Wait for **Deposit received**, then send `/start` and check that **Balance** has increased before continuing. For other funding options, see [Deposit funds](/funds/deposit).

    {/* Screenshot reuse: deposit-addresses.png */}
    <div className="cove-guide-figure">
      <Frame caption="Open Deposit and copy your own address under Deposit SOL to receive USDC on Solana.">
        <img src="/assets/guide-screenshots/deposit-addresses.png" alt="The Deposit panel with the Deposit SOL address used for USDC on Solana." width={733} height={1002} className="cove-guide-screenshot" />
      </Frame>
    </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a token">
    Paste the token's contract address or a supported token link into the bot. Select the intended chain if asked.

    Check the token address, chain, selected wallet, and any warnings. Make sure the panel is on **Buying**.

    {/* Screenshot: trade-buy-panel.png | An example Buying panel; presets and slippage are saved user settings, not recommendations. */}
    <div className="cove-guide-figure">
      <Frame caption="An example Buying panel; presets and slippage are saved user settings, not recommendations.">
        <img src="/assets/guide-screenshots/trade-buy-panel.png" alt="An example Buying panel; presets and slippage are saved user settings, not recommendations." width={752} height={795} className="cove-guide-screenshot" />
      </Frame>
    </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an amount and buy">
    For a separate confirmation like the one below, first open `/settings` → **Trade panel settings** and enable **Buy confirm**, then return to the token panel.

    Tap **\$x** to enter a custom USD amount, or choose a preset. Check the amount and wallet, then tap **Buy** with the displayed amount when ready. Read any additional warning or fee prompt before proceeding.

    <Warning>Buy confirm is off by default. Without it, choosing a preset or entering a custom amount can submit the purchase immediately.</Warning>

    {/* Screenshot reuse: trade-buy-confirm.png */}
    <div className="cove-guide-figure">
      <Frame caption="With Buy confirm enabled, the selected amount appears on the final Buy button.">
        <img src="/assets/guide-screenshots/trade-buy-confirm.png" alt="A selected buy amount and the Buy confirmation button on the token panel." width={733} height={988} className="cove-guide-screenshot" />
      </Frame>
    </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the purchase result">
    Wait for **Buy Executed** and check **Spent** and **Received**. **Spent** includes the trading fee. If the purchase is still processing, wait for its result before trying again.

    The token panel now shows your holding: **Cost**, **Worth**, and **PnL**. The screenshot below shows this updated position, not the execution receipt.

    {/* Screenshot reuse: trade-position-after-buy.png */}
    <div className="cove-guide-figure">
      <Frame caption="After a buy, the token panel shows your position's cost, current worth, and PnL.">
        <img src="/assets/guide-screenshots/trade-position-after-buy.png" alt="The token position after a purchase, showing Cost, Worth, and PnL." width={733} height={700} className="cove-guide-screenshot" />
      </Frame>
    </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your token in Positions">
    Send `/positions` or tap **Positions** in the main menu. Find your token in the list and check its **Worth** and **PnL**. Tap the token's ticker to reopen its trading panel.

    If it is missing, tap **Refresh** and check the selected profile and filters. See [View and manage positions](/features/positions) for more options.

    {/* Screenshot reuse: positions-list.png */}

      </Frame>
    </div>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If the buy does not go through

| Message | Next step |
|---|---|
| Not enough USDC | Check the profile and selected wallets, or reduce the amount |
| Scam warning | Read **Find out why** before deciding |
| Previous order still processing | Wait for its result before sending another trade |
