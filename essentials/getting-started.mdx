Cove gives you a unified balance for supported trading flows. You do not need to prepare a separate gas balance for every trade, but gas sponsorship has a cap and some deposit routes have their own requirements.

<Stepper steps={[{label: 'Start'}, {label: 'Deposit'}, {label: 'Trade'}]} currentStep={1} />

## 1. Start the bot

Open [Cove Bot](https://t.me/cove_trading_bot) in Telegram and select `Start`.

<Frame><img src="/assets/current-ui/home-menu.png" alt="Current Cove home screen with unified balance and position values" /></Frame>

The home screen shows your unified balance, positions, and actions such as Deposit, Transfer, Withdraw, Accounts, Settings, and Contact.

## 2. Deposit

Select `Deposit` and review the address and network shown in the panel before sending funds.

The current UI states that you can deposit any token, including memecoins. It also shows:

- A dedicated Deposit SOL address.
- A dedicated Deposit HyperEVM address.
- A shared Deposit EVM address for other EVM chains.
- USDC deposits that can route into the Solana trading balance.
- Solana deposits described as instant in the current panel.
- A displayed minimum of $25 on Ethereum and $1 on other chains.
- Card Top Up through MoonPay.

Some chains use smart wallets, where funds sit in a contract wallet controlled by your wallet access. Review the current Deposit panel for the exact route, asset, minimum, and fee before sending.

<Frame><img src="/assets/current-ui/deposit.png" alt="Current Cove Deposit panel with funding routes and deposit addresses" /></Frame>

## 3. Trade

Send a token contract address to the bot or open a supported token link.

<Frame><img src="/assets/current-ui/trading-buy-panel.png" alt="Current Cove Buy panel after a token contract has been resolved" /></Frame>

Review the chain, token data, account, amount, slippage, and confirmation settings. Then select a buy or sell action. Follow the receipt and open Positions to review the result.

See [Trading](/features/trading) for the panel controls.
