Cove combines wallet access controls, pre-trade checks, and execution routing. No single control removes the risks of token trading.

## Wallet access

Cove uses Privy and secure Mini App flows for wallet access and signing. A wallet can remain under your control after Cove or Privy signing access is revoked. Smart-wallet and downgrade behavior depends on the account flow. See [Accounts & Wallets](/essentials/accounts).

Do not share private keys, seed phrases, authentication codes, or export screenshots.

## Execution protections

The product includes slippage, liquidity protection, max-buy adjustment, high price-impact confirmation, token data checks, and gas surplus controls. These settings have different scopes and do not guarantee a successful or profitable trade.

Private routing and simulation can reduce certain execution risks, but they do not guarantee a fill or prevent loss.

## Token risk

Token checks can identify some contract or execution risks. They cannot prevent a legitimate token from losing value, becoming illiquid, or behaving differently after deployment. Verify the contract address and review the token and liquidity data before submitting a trade.

## Gas sponsorship

The UI describes Cove as gasless for supported flows, but sponsorship has a cap. If the network cost exceeds the sponsored amount, Cove can ask you to approve a gas surplus according to Settings, Guardrails.
