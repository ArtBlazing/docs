Security is the cornerstone of Cove. In 2026, relying solely on public mempools and standard routing is a risk few can afford. Cove architecture utilizes advanced, proprietary transaction routing and validation to preserve your capital.

## Architecture Highlights

Our security approach is proactive, operating on the transaction level *before* execution.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Self-Custodial Architecture">
    Cove operates on a strictly self-custodial basis. While we provide the interface via Telegram, your private keys are encrypted and stored locally via the Telegram Mini App environment or your imported external wallet. Cove servers never have access to your raw private keys, meaning your funds are always under your control.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="MEV Protection (Private RPCs)">
    Maximal Extractable Value (MEV) bots operate by frontrunning and sandwiching standard transactions, siphoning off your profits.
    
    Cove completely bypasses the public mempool. When you submit a trade via Cove, the transaction is routed through our network of **Private RPCs**, directly to trusted block builders. By keeping the transaction hidden from the mempool until it is included in a block, we guarantee 100% MEV protection for standard operations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Anti-Rug Mechanism">
    Honeypots, extreme taxes, and malicious contract functions are commonplace. 
    
    Cove utilizes an **Anti-Rug simulation engine**. Before your buy transaction is executed on-chain, our engine simulates the purchase *and the subsequent sale* of the token. If the token is a honeypot (cannot be sold) or has a hidden transfer tax over a predefined threshold, Cove automatically rejects the transaction and warns you.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>
**Always verify the token contract address.** While our anti-rug system provides substantial protection, it cannot prevent you from buying a legitimate (tradeable) token that simply loses value due to market mechanics. Always DYOR.
</Warning>
