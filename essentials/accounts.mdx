Cove provides instant Multi-Account control. You can generate fresh wallets for different trading strategies safely within Telegram.

## Wallet Management

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Multi-Accounts">
    Click **Accounts** on the home screen to open management settings.

    <Frame><img src="/assets/accounts_menu.png" alt="Accounts menu overview" /></Frame>

    Generate fresh wallets instantly.
    1. Click **New Account**.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/accounts_new%20account%20clicked.png" alt="Clicking New Account" /></Frame>
    2. Cove automatically provisions multiple wallets.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/accounts_3%20accs%20created.png" alt="3 Accounts created" /></Frame>
    3. Click on the new account (e.g., Account 2) to switch wallets.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/accounts_click%20on%20account%202.png" alt="Switching to Account 2" /></Frame>
    4. Click **Rename** to organize and label your wallets.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/accounts_click%20on%20rename.png" alt="Clicking rename" /></Frame>
       <Frame><img src="/assets/accounts_after%20rename.png" alt="Accounts list after renaming" /></Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Import Wallets">
    Cove supports private key imports. You can trade with an existing EVM or Solana wallet.
    
    1. Select **Import Wallet** from the Accounts menu.
    2. The chain selection menu appears. Choose a specific format (e.g., SOL only) or use global import.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/accounts_import%20wallet.png" alt="Chain Format Selection Menu" /></Frame>
    3. Cove prompts you to send your private key securely.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/import_sol%20only.png" alt="Importing SOL wallet prompt" /></Frame>
    4. Paste your key. Confirm the import in the chat interface.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/acoounts_import_confirm%20import.png" alt="Confirming the key import" /></Frame>
    5. Assign a label to surface the wallet in your Multi-Account list.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/accounts_import_account%20label.png" alt="Assigning a label to import" /></Frame>
    6. Cove confirms the wallet import.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/accounts_import_success%20.png" alt="Import Success Notification" /></Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Export Keys">
    Cove is fully self-custodial. You retain complete access to your private keys at all times.
    
    1. Select **Export Wallet** from the Accounts menu.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/accounts_menu.png" alt="Selecting Export Wallet" /></Frame>
    2. Cove opens a secure Telegram mini-app modal revealing your keys.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/accounts_export%20wallet%20menu%20mini%20app.png" alt="Export Wallet secure menu" /></Frame>
    3. Click the copy icon to copy your private key.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/accounts_export%20wallet_copy%20key.png" alt="Copying the exported private key" /></Frame>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
