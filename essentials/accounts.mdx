## Account roles

The Accounts panel shows the account label, EVM address, Solana address, balances, and active account state.

The `Primary account` receives funds from other users and future benefits shown by the product. Cove does not allow the primary account to be deleted. Use `Set as Primary` to change the primary account after reviewing the warning.

Use `Rename` to give an account a recognizable label. Use `New Account` to create another Cove account.

<Frame><img src="/assets/current-ui/account-rename.png" alt="Current Rename Account prompt with the label length limit" /></Frame>

## Import a wallet

Cove supports EVM and Solana wallet imports. The current flow lets you choose a wallet format, confirm the import in the Telegram flow, and assign a label.

1. Open `Accounts`.
2. Select `Import Wallet`.
3. Choose EVM, Solana, or the available combined option.
4. Complete the secure import flow.
5. Assign an account label.

<Frame><img src="/assets/current-ui/account-import-wallet.png" alt="Current Import Wallet prompt for choosing EVM, Solana, or both wallet types" /></Frame>

Never share a private key, seed phrase, authentication code, or other wallet secret.

## Export and access removal

Use `Export Wallet` to open Cove's secure Mini App flow. Perform exports only in the trusted product surface and store the result offline.

Some accounts use Privy signing access and smart-wallet infrastructure. Removing Cove or Privy signing access revokes Cove's ability to trade through that account. The wallet itself remains under your control.

## Delete or revoke an account

The current flow can require a Privy wallet-management Mini App and a smart-wallet downgrade or revoke step before deletion.

Before deleting an account:

1. Withdraw or transfer the remaining funds.
2. Review the account and wallet warning.
3. Complete the Privy or Cove signing-access removal step.
4. Confirm deletion in the secure product surface.

<Frame><img src="/assets/current-ui/account-revoke-signing-access.png" alt="Current Cove and Privy signing-access removal warning" /></Frame>

A deleted wallet can be re-imported if you still control it. Cove does not regain signing access until you explicitly import or reconnect it.

<Warning>
Never publish wallet addresses together with balances, usernames, private keys, seed phrases, or authentication codes.
</Warning>
