Configure your core execution settings to control your edge. 

Click the **Settings** button on the home screen to open the dashboard.

<Frame><img src="/assets/settings_menu.png" alt="Settings Menu Dashboard" /></Frame>

## Configuration Menu

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Slippage">
    Slippage protects your limit and market orders from negative price changes between submission and on-chain execution.
    
    1. Click **Slippage** from the settings menu.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/settings_slippage.png" alt="Slippage settings menu" /></Frame>
    2. Set the value to `Auto` or enter a specific custom percentage. If you trade high volatility low-cap tokens, increase the slippage limit to guarantee execution during heavy volume.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/settings_slippage_set%2020.png" alt="Slippage set to 20%" /></Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Buy Presets">
    Buy presets are quick-action buttons loaded onto the `Buy` panel. You click them to distribute capital instantly without typing amounts.

    1. Open the Settings dashboard and click **Buy Presets**.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/settings_buy%20presets.png" alt="Buy Presets Overview" /></Frame>
    2. Click **Edit** to modify a specific button value.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/settings_buy%20presets_edit.png" alt="Editing a Buy Preset" /></Frame>
    3. Type the new USDC amount. Cove relies on a Unified USDC balance to operate gaslessly across all chains.
       <Frame><img src="/assets/settings_buy%20presets_edited.png" alt="Buy Preset Successfully Edited" /></Frame>

    You can revert to the standard deployment stack at any time. Simply click **Reset to Default** to restore the original preset layout.
    
    <Frame><img src="/assets/settings_buy%20presets_reset%20to%20default.png" alt="Resetting Buy Presets to defaults" /></Frame>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
