# Cove

<img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/hero_banner.png" alt="Cove trading documentation banner" />
<img className="hidden dark:block" src="/assets/hero_banner.png" alt="Cove trading documentation banner" />

Cove is a Telegram-native trading terminal for supported on-chain markets. It combines visual buy and sell panels with positions, orders, accounts, transfers, referrals, and group links.

<CardGroup>
  <Card title="Unified balance" icon="wallet" iconType="solid">
    Deposit once and use a unified USDC balance for trading flows across the networks listed on [Supported Chains](/essentials/supported-chains).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Visual trading" icon="chart-line" iconType="solid">
    Open a token panel from a contract address or supported link, review the token details, and submit a buy or sell.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sponsored gas" icon="gas-pump" iconType="solid">
    Cove sponsors gas up to a cap. A trade that needs more can ask for an approved gas surplus from your balance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Privy wallet access" icon="shield-check" iconType="solid">
    Cove uses Privy signing access and secure Mini App flows. Review the account and wallet pages before exporting or revoking access.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What you can do

- Trade supported tokens from Telegram.
- Set limit buys, take profits, stop losses, and DCA ladders.
- Track positions, sort by Worth or Age, hide positions, and generate PnL cards.
- Trade with multiple Cove accounts.
- Deposit, transfer between Cove accounts, transfer to a Telegram user, and withdraw.
- Configure guardrails and trade panel behavior.

Start with [Getting Started](/essentials/getting-started).
