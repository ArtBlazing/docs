Cove is a Telegram trading bot. Buy and sell tokens on **Arc, Solana, Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain**, and other [supported chains](/essentials/supported-chains) with a USDC trading balance. Manage several wallets and set orders to act when a token reaches a target.

Open the [Cove Trading](https://t.me/cove_trading_bot) bot in Telegram. Send `/start` whenever you want to return to the main menu.

## How Cove fits together

| Part | What you use it for |
|---|---|
| **Telegram bot** | Trading, orders, alerts, funding, settings, and support |
| **Profiles** | Separate sets of wallets, positions, orders, and trading settings |
| **Wallets** | Hold funds; select one or several for a trade |
| **Mini Apps** | Visual positions, portfolio history, deposits, and wallet management |
| **Agent MCP** | Connect an external agent through the [MCP guide](/builders/mcp-tools) |

Your **Balance** is spendable USDC on Solana in the active profile. Stablecoins on other chains may be moving into that balance or waiting for your approval. See [Bridge and parked funds](/funds/bridge).

Cove sponsors network fees within its limits. Trading fees and occasional additional network costs still apply; see [Fees and minimums](/essentials/fees-structure).
